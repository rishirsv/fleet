# Design research

Produce evidence for a product decision. Use the [context contract](../../references/context-contract.md)
and [workflow guidance](../../references/workflows-guide.md). Choose the mode
from the request; do not turn a synthesis of supplied data into an unrelated
web scan or claim to have conducted interviews.

## Plan

Read the full [user-research command](../../references/research-planning.md).
Name the decision, participants, known uncertainty and observation that would
change the decision. Choose a method suited to the question, justify recruitment
and sample limitations, and prepare neutral questions, tasks and analysis criteria.
Include consent/privacy needs relevant to the data. Suggested counts are not
proof of representativeness or statistical power. Deliver a runnable study plan;
contacting participants or sending surveys requires the user's instruction.

## Synthesize

Read the full [research-synthesis command](../../references/research-synthesis.md).
Also read [user-research methods](../../references/research-planning.md)
for affinity mapping, journeys, jobs to be done and prioritization. Inventory
supplied sources, date range, participant/sample coverage and gaps. Supplement
with connected feedback, support, NPS or analytics sources when available and
within the requested research scope; label these additions and their provenance.
Maintain a compact evidence ledger: claim, source/locator, date, label, supporting
and conflicting evidence. Labels are observed, participant-reported, inferred,
or assumption. Quote exactly with participant identifiers that do not expose
personal data. Count distinct participants, not repeated mentions; use n/N only
when the denominator is known. Separate theme prevalence from population claims.
Show contradictory cases and limitations, then map evidence to opportunities and
prioritized next decisions. Do not invent missing interviews or customer voices.

## Scan current product problems

State the named product, audience, timeframe and scope. Search relevant public
sources and authorized internal sources when useful. Cite original reports and
dates, deduplicate repeated reports, distinguish version-specific failures from
current behavior, and separate user reports from reproduced observations.
Rank problems by impact, evidence strength and frequency signal without treating
loud complaints as representative. Separate workflow friction, reliability,
documentation and feature requests. Report access gaps and uncertainty.

## Deliver

Return the decision-relevant findings, evidence ledger/source map, limitations
and next action. Use the requested format; otherwise preserve the selected workflow's
Output structure (Deliverables for research planning) in chat, with unsupported fields marked unknown. Save reusable
findings only when requested, through `$design-context`, with sources and scope.
Follow [reporting guidance](../../references/reporting.md).
