# Design reporting

Lead with the visible result, decision, or highest-impact finding. Name elements,
quote relevant copy, and connect observations to the user's task. Use plain
language. Separate measured facts, observations, inferences and preferences.

For findings give location, evidence, impact, correction and priority. Do not
invent scores, measurements, user prevalence or compliance claims. State which
routes, viewports and interactions were inspected and which remain unverified.
For delivery link the implementation and relevant evidence. Record blockers and
material differences; do not imply publication or production readiness from a
local preview. Use the user's format; otherwise use the selected workflow's Output
structure when applicable, marking unsupported fields unknown. Omit routine
process narration. Do not add sharing prompts or approval gates to completed work.
