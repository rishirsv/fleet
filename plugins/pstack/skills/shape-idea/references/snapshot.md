# Idea Snapshot

Return this self-contained contract when the frontier is empty. Preserve the
user's language where it matters.

```md
**Idea Snapshot**

**Shared understanding**

<Two to four sentences stating what you believe the user means.>

**Decisions**

- **<plain-language decision>**: <the answer>. <Why it won and the tradeoff
  accepted. Mark answers you gave on the user's behalf.>

**Guardrails**

- **Non-negotiables:** <exact constraints, or "None surfaced">
- **Non-goals:** <what this should not become, or "None surfaced">
- **Rejected branches:** <important paths not chosen and why, or "None">
- **Assumption to test:** <the riskiest remaining assumption, or "None surfaced">

**Open or deferred**

- **<decision>**: <open or deferred>. Depends on <evidence or event>; blocks
  <what cannot safely proceed>.

Write `None` when nothing is deferred.

**What shaped it**

<Only direction-changing user input, earlier attempts, research with
citations, or prototype reactions with artifact links. Omit when none applied.>

**Next mode**

<stop, research, plan, design, document, break down, or build>: <why the result
is ready for that mode and what context must carry forward.>
```
