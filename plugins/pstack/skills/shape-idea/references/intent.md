# Intent

Keep one `intent.md` for the conversation, not one per idea, unless the user
requests separate files. Reuse it across turns; do not overwrite unrelated work.
Use the requested or established task location, or create a conversation-specific
task directory. Report the path. Save a draft early and update it as shaping proceeds.

Write a self-contained account for the next step. Preserve the user's language
where it matters. Adapt length, structure and headings to the content; use prose,
lists or tables as useful. Do not force a template, empty sections or placeholders.

Explain the problem or opportunity, desired outcome, why it matters, and who or
what is affected when relevant and known. Carry forward consequential decisions,
reasons, constraints, exclusions and tradeoffs. Distinguish accepted choices from
proposals and assumptions, including answers inferred with permission. Record
open or deferred questions and what they block. Link direction-changing evidence
or artifacts. Mark the draft ready when choices for the next step are settled
or deferred without blocking it; state that step and its context when useful.
Focus on what is wanted and why, not a full specification or implementation plan.
