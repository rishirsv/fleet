# Intent

Write a self-contained account that carries the user's intent into the next
step. Preserve their language where it matters. Adapt its length, structure,
and headings to the idea; use prose, lists, or tables as useful. Do not force a
template, empty sections, or placeholder values.

Explain the problem or opportunity, the desired outcome and why it matters,
and who or what is affected when relevant and known. Carry forward consequential
decisions and their reasons, constraints, exclusions, and tradeoffs as needed.
Distinguish accepted choices from proposals and assumptions, and identify any
choices inferred with permission. Include open or deferred questions and what
they block when relevant. Cite direction-changing evidence or link artifacts
when they matter. State the next step and the context it needs when useful.

Keep the intent focused on what is wanted and why, rather than requiring a full
specification or implementation plan. Return it in the conversation unless the
user's request or project conventions call for saving it; follow the requested
or established destination and format.
